🚀 Version 0.22.0 is out! We just shipped an update that moves the Rubik Variable font out of the main Koval UI bundle. Doing this drops the bundle size and gives you way more flexibility with your typography.

⚠️ Since Rubik powers our Podil (default) and Dnipro themes, [you'll need to manually add the font](/docs/quickstart) to your project after upgrading.
